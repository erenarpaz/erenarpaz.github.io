Waddle Out: Duck Escape

Tap Away Color Sort Puzzle · iPhone

The ducklings are stuck in a crowded pond and every one of them wants to go home to mama. Tap a duckling and it waddles straight ahead, but only if nothing is in its way.

  • Freed ducklings wait on a shelf with 5 spots. Three of the same color fly home.
  • Every level is guaranteed to be solvable.
  • Three cozy ponds: Lily Pond, Sunset Marsh and Moonlit Lake.
